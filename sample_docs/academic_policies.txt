Academic Policies: Attendance and Examinations

Students are required to maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each
subject to be eligible to sit for the semester-end examination. Students
falling short of this requirement due to documented medical reasons may
apply for condonation through the head of department.

The semester grading system is based on a combination of internal
assessments (30%), mid-semester examination (20%), and end-semester
examination (50%). Internal assessments include class tests, assignments,
and quizzes conducted throughout the semester.

Students who fail a subject in the end-semester examination are eligible
to appear for a supplementary examination, which is conducted approximately
6 weeks after the results are declared. A student may attempt a
supplementary examination a maximum of 3 times per subject.

Requests for re-evaluation of answer scripts must be submitted within
7 days of the result declaration, along with the prescribed re-evaluation
fee, through the examination cell.
