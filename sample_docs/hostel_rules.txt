Hostel Rules and Regulations

All hostel residents must return to the hostel premises by 9:30 PM on
weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Late entries must be recorded at the
security desk and require prior written permission from the warden for
entries after the curfew time.

Visitors are allowed in the common room only, between 4:00 PM and
7:00 PM. Visitors are not permitted inside individual rooms at any time.

Room maintenance requests (electrical, plumbing, furniture) should be
submitted through the hostel's online maintenance portal. Requests are
typically resolved within 48 hours for non-urgent issues, and within
6 hours for urgent issues such as water leakage or electrical faults.

Mess timings are as follows: breakfast 7:30-9:00 AM, lunch 12:30-2:00 PM,
dinner 7:30-9:00 PM. Students on medical diets can request modified meals
by submitting a doctor's note to the hostel warden's office.
