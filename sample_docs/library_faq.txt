Library Hours and Borrowing Policy

The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays, and
9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends. Students may borrow up to 5 books at a
time for a period of 14 days. Books can be renewed twice online through
the library portal if no other student has placed a hold on them.

Reference books, journals, and materials in the rare collection room
cannot be borrowed and must be used within the library premises.

Overdue books are charged a fine of 2 rupees per day per book. Fines
above 500 rupees will result in a temporary suspension of borrowing
privileges until the fine is cleared.

Students can reserve a study room in the library for group study
sessions by booking in advance through the student portal. Rooms are
available in slots of 2 hours and can be booked up to 3 days in advance.
